Sketch 001 — Dimmed Card Styles

question: how do we make “locked” cards read clearly while keeping the hazard-tape titlebar bright?

Each tab applies a different dimmed-body treatment to the same three portfolio cards: Lettermatch (live, pristine) on the left, hiRelay and BuildMeThis (dimmed) on the right. Compare the hazard tape visibility, traffic-light color, body legibility, and overall “vibe” across variants.

Current (shipped)

baseline

Today's behavior: filter: brightness(0.75) saturate(0.65) contrast(0.92) on the outer wrapper. Everything — titlebar, hazard tape, body — gets darkened. The hazard tape loses its yellow punch.

A · Filter on body only

smallest diff

Keep the brightness/saturate filter, but scope it to .win-body. Titlebar and hazard tape render at full color. The body still looks greyer than today but the card no longer reads as a mud puddle.

B · Frosted glass

premium

Body gets backdrop-filter: blur + saturate plus a warm cream scrim and a faint dot-noise overlay. Content shape still suggests itself through the frost. Strongest "don't-touch" feel.

C · Blueprint paper

metaphor-forward

Body swapped for a cream paper + faint blueprint grid + padlock watermark. Content is hidden, not blurred. Furthest from today's look but the clearest "placeholder" signal.

E · Scrim + 135° crosshatch ★

recommended

No filter. Light cream scrim + a faint 135° crosshatch (same angle as the hazard tape). Body content is still legible but visibly fenced off. The shared angle ties titlebar and body together.
